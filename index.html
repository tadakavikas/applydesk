Questions people actually ask

Is a real person sending my applications?+
Yes. A trained recruiter on our team reads each posting, adapts your resume to it, and submits it by hand. Software handles the tracking and reporting, never the judgment.
What happens when an interview comes in?+
You take it. Interview invitations go to you directly, and your recruiter flags them in your daily report. While you prepare, the application volume keeps running so your pipeline never goes quiet.
Can I set rules, like companies or locations to skip?+
Yes, and at any time. Send instructions through the portal chat: target companies, roles to skip, priority changes, salary floors. Your team applies them within one business day.
Will you ever stretch the truth on my resume?+
No. We work only with your real experience. We reframe it, sharpen it, and translate it into the keywords screeners look for, but we never invent employment history, credentials, or work authorization.
Do you guarantee I'll get hired?+
No one can honestly promise that, and we won't. What we guarantee is the volume and quality of daily applications and outreach. Hiring decisions belong to employers; keeping your name in front of them relentlessly is our job.
How does payment work?+
Pay in advance by Zelle, bank transfer, or card link. Service renews every 30 days on payment. You can cancel any upcoming month with a simple email, no notice period and no penalty.
