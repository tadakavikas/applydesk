Common questions

Straight answers

How do you improve my resume?

We work only with your real experience. We reword it with the keywords recruiters search for and measurable results, and adjust it for each specific job description. We do not invent anything.

How do you access my accounts safely?

You sign a simple service agreement and share credentials for job portals only, never your personal email. Everything we do appears in your daily report, and you can revoke access anytime.

What counts as a quality application?

A role that matches your skills, level, salary range, and work-authorization needs, applied with a resume adjusted to that job description. Each one appears on your report with a link.

Do you guarantee interviews or offers?

No, and we would be careful of anyone who does. Hiring decisions belong to employers. What we commit to is the daily work: the applications, the outreach, and a complete report you can verify.

What if I get hired quickly?

Then the plan simply ends at the end of the month. There is no lock-in and no penalty. You can cancel any month.

Who is on my team?

A dedicated recruiter runs your daily work, an operations manager reviews quality and steps in on tricky roles, and admin oversight reads every conversation. All three are visible to you in your dashboard chat.

What data do you store about me?

Your name, email, phone, target roles, portal credentials, and every application we submit on your behalf. We do not store IDs, SSN, or bank details. You can request deletion when your subscription ends.
